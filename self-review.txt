For CLAUDE.md:
- within a file, build top-down, not bottom-up
- avoid unicode characters that have clear ASCII alternatives, like →, …, 
- avoid run-on sentences.  Don't extend a sentence with parentheseis or hyphens.  Examples:
  - Summary tabs are a separate case: their rows are histogram entries, not records, so the "Showing N records …" framing doesn't apply and `event_for_line` is empty (the line→event map only exists for tabs whose lines come from real events).
- similarly, colons semicolons have their place, but don't over use them.
  - Example:
    - While a [`LongOp`] is active, the parse-stats line is replaced with a progress bar; Ctrl-C cancels and unwinds (no partial summary, no anchor change for search). 
      Just use two sentences.
    - The bar shrinks (and at very narrow widths is dropped entirely) so the numeric components are never truncated — losing the percent number is more confusing than losing bar resolution.
    - "Toggled with `p` and not persisted across sessions: it's a debugging affordance, not part of the user's view state."
- try harder to avoid unwrap/expect when you can structure stuff with `let else`, match, etc.
